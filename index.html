Questions we often get.

Is your question not listed? Send a message and you will hear from us within one business day.

Ask your question
How long does a typical executive search take?

Timelines depend on the seniority of the role and the state of the market. We agree a clear plan and milestones with you at the start, and keep you updated at every stage.

What does a retained search involve?

We map the market, assess leadership capability against what the role demands next, and engage candidates directly. You receive a considered shortlist, not a stack of CVs.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Many leadership moves are sensitive. We approach candidates discreetly and share information on a need-to-know basis throughout the process.

Which roles and sectors do you cover?

We specialise in leadership appointments for private equity investors and their portfolio companies, from Board and CEO through to functional C-suite and Director roles.

How do you support the transition after an appointment?

We stay close after an offer is accepted, helping both sides settle into the role so the appointment delivers value well beyond the first few months.

Can I speak to a partner before committing?

Yes. Get in touch and a partner will talk through your situation, the market and our approach, with no obligation.
